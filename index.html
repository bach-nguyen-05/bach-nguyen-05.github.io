About Me

Research Assistant in the AI Reasoning Lab at Arizona State University, working on the reasoning of multimodal large language models under uncertainty.

Hello! I am Bach Nguyen, an undergraduate student in Computer Science at Arizona State University and a Research Assistant in the AI Reasoning Lab, where I work with Professor Ben Zhou (ARC Lab) and PhD researchers on multimodal LLM reasoning and decision-making under uncertainty — including frameworks for handling model hallucinations. I lead a research effort on an active reasoning benchmark for multimodal LLMs that enables iterative uncertainty resolution.

My interests span Natural Language Processing, Computer Vision, and multimodal reasoning. I enjoy exploring new ideas and collaborating with talented people, and I am excited to dive into the unknowns and face any difficulties.

Back in high school, I was admitted to a specialized math class for only 30 gifted students in my city, where I immersed myself in advanced math early — linear algebra, calculus, and statistics. In my senior year, I achieved first prize in the city math competition out of 300 selected students.
